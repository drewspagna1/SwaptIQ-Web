Support

Answers to common questions. Can't find what you need? We're one email away.

How do I connect my Sleeper league?

Open SwaptIQ, go to add a league, and enter your Sleeper league ID. You can find your league ID in the Sleeper app or on sleeper.com by opening your league and copying the number from the league's URL. Your league must be created in Sleeper first — SwaptIQ reads from it, it doesn't create leagues.

Where do trade values come from?

SwaptIQ uses trusted third-party dynasty fantasy football value data to price players and picks, adjusted for your league's format (Superflex vs. 1QB, dynasty vs. redraft, TE premium, league size). We show these values directly in the app rather than hiding them behind a proprietary score.

How does the AI trade analysis work?

When you build a trade, SwaptIQ sends the trade details, underlying player values, and relevant roster/league context to generate a plain-English verdict — including the value gap, positional reasoning, and a confidence caveat. It's an analytical opinion, not a guarantee, and it's meant to inform your decision, not replace your judgment.

Why don't I see a player, or their value looks off?

Player data and values refresh periodically rather than in real time, so very recent trades, injuries, or roster moves may take a little while to show up. If a player is missing entirely or a value looks clearly wrong, let us know — we're actively refining data coverage.

Can I evaluate trades with more than two teams?

Yes. The multi-team trade tool lets you route players and picks across three or more teams and see how the trade nets out for everyone involved.

Does SwaptIQ support Yahoo or ESPN leagues?

Not yet. SwaptIQ currently supports Sleeper leagues for NFL dynasty and redraft. Additional platforms and sports are on the roadmap — join our updates by reaching out below if you'd like to be notified.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email us at the address below with your account email and we'll delete your account and associated data.

Is my Sleeper account or password ever accessed?

No. SwaptIQ connects to Sleeper leagues using a public league ID only — never your Sleeper login credentials.

Still need help?

Send us the details — league ID, what you were trying to do, and a screenshot if you have one — and we'll get back to you.
