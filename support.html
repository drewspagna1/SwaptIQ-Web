Connecting your league

How do I connect my Sleeper league?

Open SwaptIQ, go to add a league, and enter your Sleeper league ID. You can find your league ID in the Sleeper app or on sleeper.com by opening your league and copying the number from the league's URL. Your league must be created in Sleeper first. SwaptIQ reads from it, it doesn't create leagues.

How do I connect my Yahoo league?

Open SwaptIQ, go to add a league, and choose Connect Yahoo. You'll sign in through Yahoo's own screen and grant SwaptIQ read-only access, then pick a league from the list. This is available to users in the US and Canada.

How do I connect my Fleaflicker league?

Open SwaptIQ, go to add a league, and enter your Fleaflicker league ID. You'll find it in your Fleaflicker league's web address. No login is needed for public leagues.

Which platforms does SwaptIQ support? What about ESPN?

SwaptIQ supports Sleeper, Yahoo, and Fleaflicker leagues today for NFL dynasty and redraft. Yahoo league sync is available to users in the US and Canada, and connects through Yahoo's own sign-in, so SwaptIQ never sees your Yahoo password. Sleeper and Fleaflicker connect with a public league ID and no login at all. ESPN support is on the roadmap. Reach out below if you'd like to be notified.

Values and analysis

Where do trade values come from?

For football, SwaptIQ uses trusted third-party dynasty value data to price players and picks, adjusted for your league's format (Superflex vs. 1QB, dynasty vs. redraft, TE premium, league size). For baseball, players are priced on a rest-of-season value built from official league statistics. Either way, we show these values directly in the app rather than hiding them behind a proprietary score.

How does the AI trade analysis work?

When you build a trade, SwaptIQ sends the trade details, underlying player values, and relevant roster and league context to generate a plain-English verdict, including the value gap, positional reasoning, and a confidence caveat. It's an analytical opinion, not a guarantee, and it's meant to inform your decision, not replace your judgment.

Why don't I see a player, or their value looks off?

Player data and values refresh periodically rather than in real time, so very recent trades, injuries, or roster moves may take a little while to show up. If a player is missing entirely or a value looks clearly wrong, let us know. We're actively refining data coverage.

What are League Power Rankings, and do they include draft picks?

Power Rankings show every team in your league sorted by total roster value, with a breakdown by position, plus two charts: a league-wide value and age landscape, and a by-position view of every team's roster. For Sleeper and Fleaflicker leagues, each team's current and next two years of draft picks are factored into the total and shown as their own column. Draft pick ownership isn't yet available through Yahoo's league data, so Yahoo-imported teams show player values only, without a Pick column.

Trades and sports

Can I evaluate trades with more than two teams?

Yes. The multi-team trade tool lets you route players and picks across three or more teams and see how the trade nets out for everyone involved.

Does SwaptIQ do fantasy baseball?

Yes. Switch the sport to MLB and you can build and evaluate baseball trades, including multi-team ones, with players priced on rest-of-season value. MLB trades are built by searching for players rather than by importing a league, so any league format works. Because there's no MLB league import yet, MLB trades aren't saved to your trade history.

Account and privacy

Is my league account or password ever accessed?

No. SwaptIQ connects to Sleeper and Fleaflicker leagues using a public league ID only, never your login credentials for those platforms. Yahoo leagues connect through Yahoo's own official sign-in screen, and SwaptIQ never sees or stores your Yahoo password.
